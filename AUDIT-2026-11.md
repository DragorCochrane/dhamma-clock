## Site bugs fixed

1. **DST day skip (real bug).** `blockOccurrences` stepped from "now" in 24-hour increments and read the date in the group's zone. When "now" fell late in the evening before a 23-hour spring-forward day, that day was skipped entirely. Example: at 11:30pm Sat 13 Mar 2027 in New York, a daily sit showed nothing on Sun 14 Mar. It now walks calendar dates in the group's zone.
2. **`zonedToUtc` made exact.** It now resolves using the UTC offsets on either side of the target. Ambiguous fall-back times (e.g. 1:30am on 1 Nov 2026 in the US) take the first occurrence; spring-forward gap times move forward past the gap.
   - Tested over 13 zones (US ×4, London, Sydney, Perth, Brisbane, Auckland, Colombo, Lord Howe, São Paulo, Chatham), every 15 minutes from Jan 2026 to May 2027 (644k cases).
   - The only round-trip mismatches are the 54 wall times that don't exist inside a spring-forward gap.
   - Consecutive daily occurrences never skip a day or lose their 9:00 local time. This holds across the US change on 1 Nov 2026 and the UK, AU and NZ changes.
3. **2nd/4th-week listings vanished.** The directory looked only 9 days ahead, so a "2nd & 4th Tue" block often had no visible schedule line. Blocks with a weeks-of-month rule now look 40 days ahead.
4. **Stale directory.** The directory rendered once at load, so its "next …" dates went stale on a page left open (including across a DST change). It now re-renders hourly.
5. **UTC missing from the time-zone picker** in browsers whose zone list omits it. Added.
6. Cards can carry a `flag` note (⚠ re-check), and the footer explains it.

Checked in headless Chromium at desktop (New York) and phone (Sydney) sizes: no console errors, no horizontal scroll.
